Review notes

What I found when I reviewed the year, and what I would do about it.

  • Aging buckets are distorted. Receivables show −368,000 in 1–30 days against 358,800 older than 120 days, and payables show the same pattern. Payments were posted but not matched to invoices. Action: reconcile each payment to its invoice in Odoo, then reprint the aging.
  • VAT accounts were never settled. Input VAT (355,581.04) and output VAT (357,540) kept accumulating all year, and the Q3 return picked up only part of the quarter's sales because many imported lines had no tax grid. Action: put a tax grid on every sales and purchase line and post a settlement entry at the end of each quarter.
  • Odoo's cash flow report is mostly "unclassified". The accounts carry no cash flow tags. Action: tag them; until then the Excel statement above is the reliable one.
  • Depreciation started late. Assets were registered in mid-September, so only 19,401.10 was booked against a full-year charge of about 64,500. The model uses the full monthly charge and the reconciliation shows the gap.
  • The inventory account does not move with sales. Purchases go straight to cost of goods sold, so inventory turnover means nothing yet. Action: enable perpetual inventory valuation.
  • A GOSI line sits under an "unknown" vendor. Action: create GOSI as a partner and assign the payroll entries to it.
